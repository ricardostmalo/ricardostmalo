# Hey, I'm Ricardo

I'm from Panama and I build software that does boring, important work for small businesses in Latin America. Mostly AI agents these days.

I came to this sideways. I spent my first years out of college in finance (PIMCO, then a bank in Panama), got tired of watching good businesses drown in bookkeeping and WhatsApp messages, and started writing code to fix it. I haven't stopped.

**Right now** I lead product and engineering at [Boton](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/boton) (YC), where an agent named Sam reads bank statements, tax filings and invoices and does the accounting for ~115 businesses. About 79% of the ledger gets posted without a human touching it. I wrote the rules engine, the tool layer Sam runs on, and most of the bank integrations.

On the side I run [Ricky](https://getricky.ai), a WhatsApp/Instagram inbox with AI agents for companies that get thousands of customer messages a day. I built the whole thing myself; it's a private repo, but ask me and I'll show you around.

**Here on GitHub** you'll find the smaller things I open-sourced:

- [remote-whatsapp-mcp](https://github.com/ricardostmalo/remote-whatsapp-mcp) — lets Claude or ChatGPT read and send your WhatsApp from anywhere. I use it every day.
- [voices](https://github.com/ricardostmalo/voices) — agent skills that write like specific people (pg, sama, Dario, Gwern…).
- [sanlucas](https://github.com/ricardostmalo/sanlucas) — the website for my parish.

I work in Python and TypeScript, live in Postgres, and think the interesting problems in AI right now are about tools, evals and trust, not models.
